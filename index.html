Premium Activation

To activate the Premium version for $2.99:

  1. Open the watch face settings.
  2. Tap “Show Payment QR Code” and then tap “Save”.
  3. You will see a QR code and your personal activation code on your watch screen.
  4. Scan the QR code or visit kzl.io/code.
  5. Enter your activation code and complete the payment.
  6. Wait 5–10 minutes, then open the watch face settings again.

Activation may take up to 10 minutes after payment.

FAQ

I paid, but Premium is not active yet

Please wait 5–10 minutes, sync your watch with Garmin Connect, and open the watch face settings again.

I reinstalled the watch face. Do I need to pay again?

No. Your license should be restored automatically after synchronization. It may take a few minutes.

Weather is not updating

Open Garmin Connect on your phone and sync your watch. Make sure the watch face has the required permissions.

Always-On Display is not visible

Enable Always-On Display in your watch settings. Some Garmin models may have different AOD behavior.
